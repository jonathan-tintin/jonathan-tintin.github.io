Passion 01

Teaching & Tutoring

I have a passion for helping others, and one of my favorite ways to do that is through teaching. It started in high school when I began tutoring at a Kumon center, and it's something I've carried with me ever since.

In college, I worked as an undergraduate math tutor at UCSD and also tutored online through Chegg. In graduate school, I served as a Teaching Assistant for Calculus 1 and Calculus 2. Most recently, I taught Calculus as an adjunct professor at Foothill College — an experience that brought everything full circle.

There's something deeply satisfying about watching someone work through a problem they thought was impossible and come out the other side. That's what keeps me coming back to it.

Passion 02

Fitness & The Warrior Mindset

In 2020, I joined the Navy Warrior Challenge Program, a program designed for those pursuing a Navy special operations rate — SEAL, SWCC, EOD, or AIRR — as well as those on the SEAL Officer route. We trained through 2022, including through the height of COVID. We were mentored directly by a retired Navy SEAL.

I pursued the SEAL Officer route because I wanted to be in a leadership role that was part of something bigger than myself, something genuinely challenging and far outside my comfort zone. I was eventually accepted to attend SEAL Officer Assessment and Selection (SOAS). At the same time, I had just started my new role at Cisco, and I made the decision to focus on my career instead.

That chapter is behind me, but the discipline it built is not. I still train seriously and take my fitness as seriously as any professional commitment.

Passion 03

Personal Finance & Travel Cards

One of my more niche but deeply enjoyable hobbies is the world of travel credit cards and points optimization. I stay up to date on card benefits, sign-on bonuses, point valuations, and redemption strategies the way some people follow sports. It's part personal finance, part puzzle.

My portfolio spans 19 cards across six banks, broken into three categories: premium travel cards like the Amex Platinum, hotel co-branded cards like the Chase Ritz-Carlton card, and airline co-branded cards like the Chase United Quest card. Each card has a job, and I make sure it's doing it.

On the redemption side, I focus on two things: business and first class international flights using transferable points, and free night awards from hotel co-branded cards to keep travel costs down. The goal is never to spend more — it's to get significantly more value out of what I'm already spending.
